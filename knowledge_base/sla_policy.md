# Service-Level Agreement Policy

For this capstone, the target first-response times are P1 within 15 minutes, P2 within 1 hour, P3 within 8 business hours, and P4 within 2 business days. Target resolution times are P1 within 4 hours, P2 within 8 hours, P3 within 3 business days, and P4 within 5 business days. These are illustrative project policies, not verified official Ola commitments; agents should communicate targets rather than guarantee resolution.
