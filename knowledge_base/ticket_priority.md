# Ticket Priority Policy

Support tickets are classified as P1 Critical, P2 High, P3 Medium, or P4 Low according to customer impact and urgency. P1 covers widespread outages or critical service failures, P2 covers major functionality affecting multiple users, P3 covers limited functionality issues, and P4 covers general questions or minor inconveniences. Agents must record the observed impact and assign priority based on evidence rather than customer language alone.
