# Escalation Matrix

Escalate P1 incidents immediately to the incident manager and technical on-call team. Escalate P2 incidents to the support lead when progress is blocked or the target response or resolution time is at risk. P3 and P4 tickets should follow the normal support queue unless repeated failures, safety concerns, or a policy exception requires escalation. Record the escalation reason, timestamp, current owner, and next action in the ticket.
